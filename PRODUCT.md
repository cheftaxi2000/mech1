# Product

**What it is.** An interactive companion to first-semester ETH Zürich courses, Mechanik I first, plus Analysis 1, Linear Algebra 1, Chemistry and Computer Science 1. Every lesson follows the official lecture notes page by page. It explains each page in plain words, draws every geometric idea as an interactive or step-animated figure, and keeps what the lecturer wrote visibly apart from what the site adds.

**Who uses it.** One first-semester engineering student, studying for one to two hours at a time, mostly on an iPad in landscape and sometimes on a laptop or phone. Bilingual (German/English); Computer Science is English-only.

**The job of every screen.** Make one page of the lecture understood on first reading:
- the reader always knows where they are (subject → chapter → lesson → section);
- the reader can tell at a glance what is quoted from the Skript (with its page) and what is explanation, a supplement, or a common misconception;
- the figures and formulas carry the explanation, and the text between them stays calm.

**Surface mode.** Read (long-form study). The landing and subject pages are indexes, still Read rather than Persuade.

**Non-negotiables** (see CLAUDE.md): never invent what the lecturer said; lecturer's notation; all math in KaTeX; two complete languages; figures follow the physics colour code (x blue, y amber, z green, ρ teal, angles purple, points red); no questions inside chapters; orange misconception boxes stay.

**Tone.** Serious, precise, quiet. A well-typeset technical book that happens to move, not an app that wants attention.
