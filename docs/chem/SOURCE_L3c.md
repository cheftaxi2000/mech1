## Folie 7 (p. 5) — Nonidealities in Gases: Van der Waals Equation
- (P + n²a/V²)(V − nb) = nRT. TABLE 10.3 Van der Waals Constants for Gas Molecules (a in L²·kPa/mol², b in L/mol): He 3.46 / 0.02370; Ne 21.4 / 0.0171; Ar 135 / 0.0322; Kr 235 / 0.0398; Xe 425 / 0.0510; H2 24.7 / 0.0266; N2 141 / 0.0391; O2 138 / 0.0318.
- Handschrift (Meaning): • The measured pressure is lower than the ideal gas law predicts due to interactions, so we add n²a/V² term to P. Molecules attract each other and strike container walls with less force. • The actual container volume is larger than the molecules experience. Some of the volume is now occupied by other molecules. Thus, we subtract nb term from V.
